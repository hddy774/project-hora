ALPHA EXCHANGE v1.5.0 — reactive market, governance, lifecycle and performance
2026-10-05 UTC

IMPLEMENTATION
Retail reacts to observed price trends/volume and does not use fundamentals,
valuation, dividends or institution abilities. Institutions combine valuation,
trend/news, portfolio risk and execution cost; mostly use passive limit prices.
No institution profit or retail loss is guaranteed by forced transfers.
New companies issue 2,000 shares: treasury 1,000 / physical bank inventory 1,000.
Existing saved physical ownership is preserved. Investors acquire shares with cash.
Bank stability orders reserve actual cash/stock, respecting lending obligations.
Monthly AI votes weight actual shareholder positions, exclude treasury and duplicate
stock-loan rights, and change revenue/cost/investment/dividend/repayment policies.
Companies, institutions and retail settle insolvency; replacement capital is funded
from existing economic accounts. Distinct company IDs/investor generations retain
past losses, names, statements and complete failure archives in SQLite and ZIP.
Market/sector cumulative volume and turnover do not reset with company replacements.
Native sector tabs, governance and bankruptcy screens complement ownership UI.

PERFORMANCE / STORAGE
Precomputed institution signals, one-pass financial/performance rollups and batched
wage/consumption journal entries reduce repeated work. Compact v6 state omits unused
retail display snapshots and zero arrays; genuine nonzero positions/finances persist.
PrepareSave separates every encoded mutable field before background serialization.
Original hourly records remain immutable; display queries cap points with extrema
of price, return, cap, cash, equity and new cohort investment income preserved.
Old v5 hourly/daily JSON is never re-encoded on DB load/import. A native upgrade
found the previous checksum collision. New attach queues only missing records.
Contradictory explicit history/event/lifecycle writes still roll back atomically.
DB/ZIP schema remains additive version 5; state JSON is version 6.
