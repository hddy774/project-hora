FINANCIAL INVARIANTS
Cash: participants + companies + bank + government + exchange + real economy
      = initial system cash. Market-value change does not create cash.
Shares: participant longs + bank inventory + founders + treasury = issued shares.
Outstanding = issued - treasury. Float = outstanding - founder holdings.
Short stock liability and unpaid replacement dividends are separate obligations.
Participant opening equity + cumulative income = current net equity;
opening cash + cumulative cash flow = current cash. Company monthly cash-flow,
equity-change and balance statements reconcile, including capital actions.

DEPLOYMENT / SIGNING
GitHub Actions builds from a merged v1.3.0 tag, reruns engine/art checks, signs,
verifies app ID/version/certificate, uploads nine release assets, downloads every
asset and checks SHA-256 before publishing as latest. Source ZIP is git archive
of tagged commit. Source/keys/build outputs stay out of development/main content.
User explicitly requested deployment without existing signing key. Manual dispatch
uses generate_signing_key=true; temporary RSA-2048 key is removed on exit and never
included in artifacts, caches or repository. Public certificate/build source/run
are recorded in SIGNING-INFO.txt. Recovery reuses the verified signed artifact.
A fresh signing identity cannot update prior releases; uninstalling deletes private
data. v1.3 exports portable DB/manifest/CSV ZIPs for future reinstallations. v1.2
has no export UI; deleted previous saves cannot be recreated by the new app.
Closed app time is not simulated. Abrupt termination can lose uncommitted tail
progress and records together; successfully committed history remains continuous.
