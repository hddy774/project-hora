v1.1.0 institutional representatives

100 original anime-style adult character illustrations: women IDs 001–070,
men IDs 071–100. Generated with the image generation tool using the user's
reference illustration for painterly detail, hair, lighting and atmosphere.
All characters wear modern clothing in modern urban settings.

The 100 illustrations are packed as ten 5×2 PNG atlases, each 1536×1024.
No portrait is reused for another institution. The manifest gives each
individual's ID, name, gender, age, pixel rectangle and source SHA-256.
Canvas draws the specified rectangle; small avatars use a face crop and
profile cards display the full portrait. Original atlas files remain intact.
Approximate individual resolution is 307×512 pixels; row boundaries in the
manifest follow the generated compositions. No online resource is needed.

Files: src/AlphaExchange.Android/Assets/portraits/portraits-01.png ... 10.png
Manifest: src/AlphaExchange.Android/Assets/portraits/manifest.json
Runtime: Portraits.cs and AlphaExchange.Core/Representatives.cs
Validation: python3 scripts/check-artwork.py

The v1.0 arena illustration is retained on the lobby. Old SVG robot avatars
are retained as historical source art; institutional profiles now use the
100 newly generated human representatives.
